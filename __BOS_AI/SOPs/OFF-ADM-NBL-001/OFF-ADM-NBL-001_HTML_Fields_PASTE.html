ℹ Why This SOP Exists

Non-billable time is hours a teammate worked on a billable job that the crew lead decided not to charge — an extra man, a customer on a set budget. BOS keeps those hours on the teammate's record and leaves them off Total Time, so the labor on the invoice is right without office cleanup. This SOP covers how the office reads those calls before invoicing, how a supervisor reviews them for patterns, and the one correct way to change a call — so no invoice goes out with the wrong hours and no hour is given away unseen.

✓ Applies To

  • Office staff who invoice work orders from the billing views
  • Supervisors who review crew hours and the Unbilled Hours report
  • Any correction of a Non-billable flag or reason on a time entry
  • All work order types that bill labor by the hour

✕ Does Not Apply To

  • The crew lead's call at sign-off — see WEB-SYS-SGN-001
  • Overhead work (shop, dump runs, equipment) — separate procedure to follow
  • Pay and compensable hours — a non-billable hour is still a paid hour
  • Pricing, rates and billing adjustments for anything other than hours

field_sop_responsibilities — Rules & Responsibilities

field_sop_responsibilities
Switch to Source view in the CKEditor toolbar, then paste.
MUST
  • Before invoicing, office must read the Non-billable column on the billing view and, where it is not empty, the Hours cards on the work order. Total Time is the billable hours; Non-billable Time is the rest.
  • Office must invoice from Total Time and Labor as BOS shows them — the non-billable hours are already out of both.
  • A correction must be made on the time entry (click the hour card → Edit Time Entry) while the work order is Complete: tick or untick Non-billable, pick the reason, Save. BOS recomputes Total Time, Labor and the work order total.
  • If the work order is already Invoiced or Paid, office must first move it back to Complete with a status update, make the correction, then re-invoice — the same path as a material correction. BOS refuses the change otherwise.
  • Every correction must carry a reason. The office person's name and the time are stamped automatically and shown on the card.
  • Supervisors must review the Unbilled Hours block on the Teammate Variance page weekly — by reason and by teammate — and raise patterns with the crew lead.
  • Office must tell the crew's supervisor before changing a crew lead's call.
MUST NOT
  • Office must not use Billing Adjustment to take a man's hours off an invoice. It carries dollars, not hours — the unbilled time disappears from every report and nobody can see where the hours went.
  • Office must not edit hours or flags on an Invoiced or Paid work order in place. Move it back to Complete first; the change is tracked.
  • Office must not remove a crew lead's Non-billable call without the supervisor knowing — it was made on site by the person who saw the job.
  • Nobody may hand-edit the "set by" and "set at" stamps; they are written by BOS and are the record of who decided.
  • Crew members cannot change the flag — only supervisor and office roles see the checkbox on the time entry form. Do not ask a crew member to do it.

Overview

1. ReadNon-billable column on the billing view; cards on the WO.
2. JudgeCall looks right → invoice as shown.
3. CorrectEdit Time Entry while Complete; reason required.
4. ReviewWeekly Unbilled Hours by reason and teammate.

Pre-Checks

  • The work order is Complete — not In Progress (the crew has not finished) and not yet Invoiced.
  • You know what the customer agreed (budget, set price) from the contract, estimate or notes.

Steps

1Read the billing viewOffice
  • Open the billing view you normally invoice from. The Non-billable column sits right after Total Time and is blank when nothing was marked.
  • A value there means a crew lead made a call on that job — open the work order before invoicing.
2Read the work orderOffice
  • In the Hours section each time entry is a card. A non-billable card is grey with a Non-billable badge, the reason, who set it and when; its hours are struck through.
  • The Total line reads "X hrs · Y hrs non-billable". Total Time on the work order equals the billable hours; Non-billable Time equals Y.
3Invoice as shown — when the call is rightOffice
  • Labor was computed on the billable hours at completion. Invoice normally; nothing to adjust.
  • Mention the non-billable hours in Billing Notes only if the customer was promised a credit in writing.
4Correct — when the call is wrongOffice / Supervisor
  • Tell the crew's supervisor what you are changing and why.
  • Click the hour card → Edit Time Entry. Tick or untick Non-billable, pick the Non-billable reason, Save.
  • Back on the work order, confirm Total Time, Non-billable Time, Labor and the work order total changed as expected. BOS recomputes on save.
5Correct an invoiced jobOffice
  • Add a status update moving the work order back to Complete.
  • Make the correction as in step 4.
  • Re-invoice, and note the reason for the re-issue in Billing Notes.
6Weekly reviewSupervisor
  • Open the Teammate Variance page for the week. The Unbilled hours block shows the total, the split by reason and the top teammates; the table has a Non-billable column per person.
  • Open a teammate's detail page to see the NB badge on individual entries.
  • Raise patterns with the crew lead: the same man marked daily, "Other" used often, or budget jobs never marked.

✕ Billing Adjustment is not for hours

In 2026 the office used Billing Adjustment on 201 work orders, 57 of them negative. Those adjustments carry no hours, so nobody can see which man's time was given away or why. Use the Non-billable flag; it keeps the hours and the reason.

⚠ Invoiced means locked

BOS refuses any change to a time entry whose work order is Invoiced or Paid. Moving the work order back to Complete first is deliberate: the change is tracked, and the invoice is re-issued on purpose, not drifted.

Quality Checks

  • Total Time + Non-billable Time equals the hours on the cards for every work order you invoice.
  • Labor on the invoice matches Total Time × the rate — no manual recalculation.
  • No work order this week carries a Billing Adjustment that should have been a Non-billable flag.
  • Every correction shows a reason and a name on the card.

Completion

  • Invoice the work order as usual.
  • When a correction changed the invoice, record what changed in Billing Notes.
  • Supervisor: note patterns raised with crew leads; they feed the monthly review of unbilled hours.

Do / Don't

✕ Don't✓ Do
Knock dollars off with Billing Adjustment because "we had too many guys"Have the extra man's time marked Non-billable · Over-staffed so the hours are kept and visible
Edit the time entry on an invoiced work orderMove the work order back to Complete, correct, re-invoice
Quietly undo a crew lead's callTell the supervisor first; the call was made on site
Ask the crew lead to fix it from his phone next weekCorrect it yourself while the job is Complete — the office and supervisors hold the checkbox
