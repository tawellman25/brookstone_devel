ℹ Why This SOP Exists

Approved reviews are published on the website — on the Reviews page and on the page of the service they praise — in the customer's own words and under the name we show. A review has two image fields that behave in opposite ways: Public image goes on the website exactly as uploaded; Scan of the original note is seen by the office only. A handwritten card carries a signature, often an address or phone number, that the customer never agreed to publish. This SOP exists so the words get typed, the card stays private, and nothing reaches the public page that the customer did not say or agree to.

field_sop_responsibilities — Rules & Responsibilities

Paste this HTML into
field_sop_responsibilities
Switch to Source view in the CKEditor toolbar, then paste.
MUST
  • The words of a review must be typed into Testimony, even when the original is handwritten. The published text is what customers, search engines and screen readers read; a picture of text is invisible to both.
  • A photo of a handwritten card must go into Scan of the original note, never into Public image. The scan is office-only inside BOS.
  • Anything placed in Public image must already have the signature, address, phone number and full name cropped or blurred — it publishes exactly as uploaded.
  • The name in Testimonial by must be what the customer agreed to show. First name and initial, or first name and town, unless they said otherwise. It appears under the quote.
  • The office must set Testimony of which Services to the exact service — the review appears on that service's page and nowhere higher up.
  • Only a review the office has read in full must be set to Approved. Submissions arrive as Pending; nothing is public until Approved.
  • Edits must be limited to spelling and obvious slips. The sense, the praise and the complaints stay the customer's.
MUST NOT
  • Do not upload the card photo as the Public image "so people can see it is real". That is how a signature gets published.
  • Do not type the customer's full name, address or phone number anywhere public — Testimony, Testimonial by or an image.
  • Do not rely on the "Front Promoted" tick to hide a review. Only Status controls the Reviews page; Front Promoted affects service pages only.
  • Do not copy or share the scan's file link. The scan is hidden in BOS, but the file itself sits on the public file store — anyone with the exact link could open it until that is changed.
  • Do not publish an AI transcript unread. Fix every [?] and read the whole thing against the card first.

Overview

1. ReceiveCard, email, /review form or job QR → Pending.
2. EnterType Testimony; name; exact service; scan to the office-only field.
3. ImagePublic image only if cropped/blurred — or none.
4. ApproveRead it whole; Status = Approved.
5. CheckIt shows on /about-us/reviews and the service page.

Pre-Checks

  • You have the customer's words in front of you, complete.
  • You know which single service the review is about.
  • You know how the customer wants to be named.

Steps

1Open ReviewsOffice
  • Office ▸ Reviews (/admin/office/reviews) — all reviews; tabs Pending and Approved. Submissions from the /review form and the job QR appear here as Pending.
  • To enter a paper or emailed review, add a new testimonial from this page.
2Type the reviewOffice
  • Testimony: the customer's words, typed in full. Spelling fixes only.
  • Testimonial by: the name as agreed — e.g. "Karen M., Golden". This shows under the quote.
  • Testimony of which Services: the exact service (Repair, not Sprinkler System). The review shows on that service's page.
  • Office-only fields: Customer (their account, if any), Work order (when the QR carried it), Submitter email (never published; for a thank-you or to verify).
3Handwritten card or letterOffice
  • Photograph or scan the card and attach it to Scan of the original note (several files allowed: second page, envelope). This field is never shown to the public.
  • Type the words into Testimony yourself, or use Read the note in the list's Actions menu (shown only when a scan is attached). It drafts a transcript into Testimony and the signer into Testimonial by, marks unreadable words [?], and leaves out the signature, address and phone.
  • Read the draft against the card. Fix every [?]. These publish as the customer's own words.
4Public image — only when it is safeOffice
  • Most reviews need no image. Leave Public image empty.
  • If a photo of the card should appear publicly: in Read the note, choose the line for Blur everything below this point and check the live preview (the default 70% is there because 80% left a signature readable). Tick Use this blurred image as the Public image only when the preview shows no signature, address, phone or full name.
  • For a PDF or a type the tool cannot preview, crop the image yourself before uploading, or leave Public image empty.
5ApproveOffice
  • Read the whole review as it will publish. Set Status to Approved and save. A review created by anyone outside the office is forced back to Pending on save until the office approves it.
  • Leave Front Promoted ticked (the default) if the review may appear on its service page; untick it to keep it off service pages while still on the Reviews page.
6Check the public resultOffice
  • Open /about-us/reviews — newest first — and the service's own page. Confirm the words, the name and the absence of anything private.
  • If the review is about a job, the office may thank the customer using Submitter email; that address is never published.

✕ Two image fields, opposite behaviour

Public image renders on the website exactly as uploaded. Scan of the original note is office-only. A card photo in the wrong field publishes a customer's signature and often an address. When in doubt, no public image at all.

⚠ The scan file is private in BOS, not on disk

BOS hides the scan from everyone outside the office, but the uploaded file is stored on the public file store, so the exact file link would open for anyone who had it. Never copy or send that link. Moving scans to private storage is logged as deferred work.

ℹ Where a review appears

The Reviews page shows every Approved review, newest first. A service page shows only Approved reviews whose service is exactly that page and whose Front Promoted tick is on. The home-page strip is not built.

Quality Checks

  • Testimony holds typed words — no review is an image only.
  • No Public image shows a signature, address, phone number or full name.
  • Every Approved review names one exact service and shows on that service's page.
  • The Pending tab is empty at the end of the week.

Completion

  • Approved review visible on /about-us/reviews and the service page.
  • Card filed, scan attached to the office-only field, customer thanked if appropriate.
