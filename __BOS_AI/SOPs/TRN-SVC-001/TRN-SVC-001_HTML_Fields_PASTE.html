ℹ Why This SOP Exists

Every service we sell has one page on the website. The public sees the sales copy. A logged-in crew member opening the same page sees something else: the Crew / Training Description — how we do that service, step by step, in our way — written by the office and never shown to the public. This SOP exists so crews know that the service pages are their training reference, how to reach them, how to read them, and what must never be done with them.

field_sop_responsibilities — Rules & Responsibilities

Paste this HTML into
field_sop_responsibilities
Switch to Source view in the CKEditor toolbar, then paste.
MUST
  • A crew member must be logged in to BOS to see the crew content. Logged out, or on a client account, the same address shows the public page.
  • Before doing a service for the first time this season, a crew member must read that service's page in full and ask the crew lead about anything unclear.
  • A crew member who finds a service page with no crew text, or text that is wrong, must tell the office or the supervisor the same day.
  • The office must write crew copy on the term's edit form, in the Teammate View tab, field Crew / Training Description — never in the public field.
  • The office must check coverage on Office ▸ Content coverage before the season: every active service with an EMPTY crew description is a gap to fill.
  • Margins, costs and credentials must go in Office Only Notes, never in crew copy. Crew copy carries no prices; crews use the estimator.
MUST NOT
  • Do not screenshot, print or forward a crew page to a customer or post it anywhere. The crew body is internal.
  • Do not paste crew text into the public description field "to be thorough". The public field publishes.
  • Do not treat the crew page as optional reading because you have done the job before — it is where the office records changes to how we do the work.

field_sop_steps — Steps & Procedures

Paste this HTML into
field_sop_steps
Switch to Source view in the CKEditor toolbar, then paste.

Overview

1. Log inSame website, same address — logged in you see the crew view.
2. Find the serviceServices menu → the service → its sub-services.
3. ReadCrew / Training Description, Department, Service Code.
4. Ask / reportUnclear or missing → crew lead or office.
5. OfficeWrite in the Teammate View tab; check Content coverage.

Pre-Checks

  • You are logged in (your name shows in the site header).
  • You have the right page: the service, or its exact sub-service (Repair, not just Sprinkler System).

Steps

1Reach the pageCrew member
  • Log in to BOS. Use the Services menu on the website, two levels deep (e.g. Sprinkler System → Repair), or open /services/… directly. Each service page lists its sub-services as cards under Our … Services.
  • The address is the same one customers use. Your role decides what the page shows.
2Read the crew viewCrew member
  • Logged in as a teammate you see: the title and subtitle, the Crew / Training Description — the "how we do it" body — plus Department and Service Code (the three-letter code used in SOP codes, e.g. SCU, BFT).
  • No hero image, no prices, no "book now" button: the crew page has nothing to sell. Supervisors and office staff see a different layout with collapsible Public View / Crew View / Office Admin sections.
3Use itCrew member
  • Read it before your first job of the season on that service and whenever the crew lead says the method changed.
  • Something unclear, missing or wrong: tell your crew lead or the office the same day. A page whose crew body is empty shows as EMPTY on the office's coverage report.
4Write or fix crew copyOffice / admin
  • Edit the service term. In the Teammate View tab fill Crew / Training Description — "How we do it — crew training content. Never public." The public copy lives in the Public View tab; margins and credentials in Office Only Notes.
  • Check your work on the page itself: as office you see the Crew View section collapsed under the public copy. Log in as a teammate account, or ask a crew member, to see exactly what crews see.
  • Office ▸ Content coverage (/admin/office/content-coverage) lists every term with its public and crew description status — fill the EMPTY ones before the season.

✕ Internal content on a public address

The crew body is kept from the public by your login, not by a separate page. Anything you copy out of it — a screenshot, a printout, a forward — is no longer protected. Keep it in BOS.

ℹ Why the same page

One address for everyone means the office maintains one page per service, and a crew member can open the exact page a customer is looking at. The role, not the link, chooses the view.

⚠ Supervisors do not see the crew layout

A supervisor who is also on a crew sees the office layout, with the crew text inside the collapsible Crew View section. That is expected.

Quality Checks

  • Every active service shows a non-empty Crew / Training Description on the coverage report.
  • Crew members can describe how we do a service after reading its page, without a verbal walkthrough.
  • No crew text appears on the public page (check logged out).
  • No prices or margins in crew copy.

Completion

  • Crew: read before the first job of the season; questions asked.
  • Office: coverage report shows no EMPTY crew descriptions for active services.

Do / Don't

✕ Don't✓ Do
Text a screenshot of the crew page to a customer who asked how we do itTell them in your own words, or send them the public page link
Skip the page because you did the job last yearRead it — that is where method changes are written
Write the crew method in the public field so it is "complete"Teammate View tab → Crew / Training Description
Put the margin in the crew textOffice Only Notes
