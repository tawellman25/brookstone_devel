ℹ Why This SOP Exists

Hours on a work order come from clock entries and nothing else — billing adds up every person's entry. The green button records your own time. The crew clock lets the crew lead clock everyone on site in and out in one press, each person getting their own entry. This SOP exists so every hour worked is on the job it was worked on, under the right name, started and ended when it actually happened. Since 2026-11-13 the crew clock also remembers the morning crew: after lunch, or at the next property, the people who worked earlier today and are clocked out are already ticked — a default to check, not a record.

✓ Applies To

  • Every teammate who works on a work order, on any crew
  • Crew leads and foremen using Clock In Crew and Clock Out Crew
  • Lunch breaks, moving between properties during the day, late arrivals and early finishes

✕ Does Not Apply To

  • Deciding whose time is billed to the customer — WEB-SYS-SGN-001 (sign-off)
  • Correcting another person's entry after the fact — supervisor or office, OFF-ADM-NBL-001
  • Shop time, dump runs and other overhead work (procedure to follow)
  • Work orders already invoiced or paid — they are locked to time entry; the office corrects those

MUST
  • Every teammate must clock in when they start on the job and clock out when they stop — including for lunch. The entry is the record; memory is not.
  • Every teammate must clock in on the work order they are actually working. Open the right property before pressing the button.
  • The crew lead must tick only people who are physically on site. Clock In Crew is an attestation under your name: your location is stamped on every entry and the note records that you pressed the button.
  • The crew lead must check the pre-ticked list before confirming after lunch or at the next property: untick anyone who went home, tick anyone who arrived. BOS ticks from today's entries; it does not know who came back.
  • Anyone still clocked in on a previous job must clock out there first. BOS blocks the single button and lists the open entries; the crew clock greys the person out and skips them.
  • A long entry (over 8 hours, 14 on long-job types) must be confirmed only when it is real. If the time is wrong, fix the entry instead of confirming it.
  • Every teammate must report a wrong entry the same day to the crew lead or office, while the work order is still open.
MUST NOT
  • Do not clock in for someone else with your own green button. Use Clock In Crew, so BOS records it as a crew action by you.
  • Do not tick a person who has not arrived yet, or who left — "they will be here in a minute" is a false entry until they are.
  • Do not leave the crew clocked in through lunch to save a press. Clock out for lunch and back in after; the pre-select makes it two taps.
  • Do not use the crew clock to rebuild yesterday. Missed days are an office correction.
  • Do not clock in on an invoiced or paid work order. BOS refuses; tell the office if a correction is needed.

Overview

1. Clock InYourself: green button. Crew: Clock In Crew → tick → Clock In Selected.
2. WorkOne entry per person. Early finisher clocks out alone; late arrival clocks in alone.
3. Lunch / next jobClock Out Crew. Clock In Crew again — the morning crew is already ticked.
4. Clock OutClock Out, or Clock Out Crew. Confirm a long entry only if real.
5. Sign-offCrew lead closes the job — WEB-SYS-SGN-001.

Pre-Checks

  • You are on the work order page for the property you are at (check the address or nickname in the title).
  • You are not still clocked in on an earlier job today.
  • Crew lead: you can see everyone you are about to tick.

Steps

1Clock yourself inEvery teammate
  • On the work order page press the green Clock In button. Your entry opens and the work order moves to In Progress for the office.
  • If BOS says you are still clocked in elsewhere, it lists those entries. Clock out of each one (or set its end time), then press Clock In on this WO.
2Clock the crew inCrew lead
  • Press the blue Clock In Crew button. The Who is on site? list shows your crew type, anyone scheduled, and anyone already clocked in.
  • Tick everyone on site. Greyed rows cannot be ticked: (already clocked in) here, or (clocked in on another job) — that person clocks out there first.
  • Press Clock In Selected. BOS opens one entry per person with the same start time, your location on each, and a note that you pressed the button.
  • Anyone on site who is not on the list clocks in on their own with the green button.
3Lunch, and moving to the next propertyCrew lead
  • Press Clock Out Crew when the crew stops. Every open entry on this job closes; someone who clocked out early keeps their own end time.
  • After lunch — on the same job or the next one — press Clock In Crew. The people who worked earlier today and are now clocked out are already ticked, each with their clock-out time, e.g. (out 11:32 AM). The line above the list says how many: 3 people from earlier today are already ticked. Untick anyone who has not come back.
  • Untick whoever went home. Tick a new arrival. Then Clock In Selected.
  • On a new job the pre-select is your crew — the people you clocked in earlier today. On the same job it is everyone who worked here today, whoever clocked them in.
4Clock outEvery teammate / Crew lead
  • Your own time: press Clock Out. The crew: Clock Out Crew closes every open entry on the job.
  • If an entry is over the limit (8 hours; 14 on long-job types) BOS names the people and the hours and asks you to confirm. Press OK only if those hours are real. Otherwise cancel and fix the start or end time on the entry.
5Check the Hours sectionCrew lead
  • The Hours section on the work order shows one card per person with start and end. Everyone who worked today should have a card; nobody should have two open.
  • A missing person clocks in now, or is added through the roster at sign-off (WEB-SYS-SGN-001). Your own entry you can edit; anyone else's is a supervisor or office correction.

⚠ Pre-ticked is a default, not a record

BOS ticks the morning crew from today's time entries. It cannot know who went home at lunch or who was borrowed by another crew. Read the list before you press Clock In Selected — each tick is you saying that person is here.

✕ One open entry per person

A person clocked in on another job is greyed out and skipped, even if ticked. A second open entry would bill the same hour on two work orders. Clock them out on the other job first.

ℹ Your location is on every crew entry

The crew clock stamps the crew lead's GPS position on each person's entry and records who pressed the button. It is an attestation, and it is how the office tells one crew action from five separate clock-ins.

Quality Checks

  • The work order shows In Progress after the first clock-in.
  • One Hours card per person on site; no card for anyone who was not.
  • After lunch, the same people are back on the clock within a minute of starting — not at the end of the day from memory.
  • No long-entry confirmation was pressed to hide a forgotten clock-out.

Completion

  • At the end of the job the crew lead signs off (WEB-SYS-SGN-001); forgotten clock-outs are confirmed there.
  • Report a wrong entry the same day; the office or supervisor corrects it while the work order is still open (OFF-ADM-NBL-001).

Do / Don't

✕ Don't✓ Do
Leave everyone clocked in through lunchClock Out Crew at lunch, Clock In Crew after — the crew is pre-ticked
Press Clock In Selected without reading the listUntick who went home, tick who arrived, then confirm
Clock yourself in on the next property while still open on the lastClock Out Crew before you drive off
Confirm a 13-hour entry because the dialog is in the wayCancel and fix the end time; confirm only real long days
