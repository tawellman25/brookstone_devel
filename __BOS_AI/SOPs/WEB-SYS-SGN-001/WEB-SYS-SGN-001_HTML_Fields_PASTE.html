ℹ Why This SOP Exists

The sign-off form is where a crew lead closes a job in BOS. It confirms every crew member's hours for that work order and decides whose time is charged to the customer. This SOP exists so the hours we bill match the people the job actually needed, decided by the one person who knows — the crew lead, at the moment the work ends — and recorded under that person's name. Hours marked non-billable still count as hours worked for the teammate; only the invoice leaves them out.

✓ Applies To

  • Every crew lead or foreman who saves a sign-off form in BOS (Clean-Up, Landscape, Irrigation, Spray and Fertilizing crew sign-offs, and the Mowing tasks form)
  • Every crew member's time on that work order, whether they clocked in themselves, were clocked in by the crew clock, or are added through the roster at sign-off
  • All services and all work order types that bill labor by the hour

✕ Does Not Apply To

  • Office corrections after the work order is Complete — see OFF-ADM-NBL-001
  • Setting hourly rates, trip fees or prices — business settings, office only
  • Invoicing — the office invoices from the billable hours this form produces
  • Shop time, dump runs and other overhead work (separate procedure to follow)

field_sop_responsibilities — Rules & Responsibilities

field_sop_responsibilities
Switch to Source view in the CKEditor toolbar, then paste.
MUST
  • The crew lead must save the sign-off the same day the work ends — on site or before leaving the truck.
  • The crew lead must tick every person who worked on site in the crew roster (Who was on site with this truck / Those on Crew) before saving. A person left off the roster has no hours on the job.
  • The crew lead must confirm or correct every end time BOS lists under Confirm end times for forgotten clock-outs. The prefilled time is an estimate, not a record.
  • The crew lead must leave everyone billed unless that person's time should not be charged to the customer: an extra man the job did not need (Over-staffed), or a customer who agreed a set price or budget (Customer budget).
  • The crew lead must pick a reason for every Non-billable tick: Over-staffed, Customer budget, Training, Rework or Other. BOS refuses the save without one.
  • The crew lead must make the call for the men on site at the time the work was performed — not later from memory, and not for someone else's crew.
  • If a call was wrong, the crew lead must tell the supervisor or office the same day so it can be corrected while the work order is still Complete.
MUST NOT
  • The crew lead must not mark time non-billable to hide a slow day, a late start or a mistake on the crew's side. Rework is a reason; a slow morning is not.
  • The crew lead must not sign off while anyone is still clocked in on another job without checking — BOS blocks the save and names them for a reason.
  • The crew lead must not leave Who gets billed untouched on a job where the customer agreed a budget. Unbilled hours that are never marked cannot be seen by anyone and are lost.
  • The crew lead must not discuss billing, hours or price with the customer. The office invoices.
  • The crew lead must not change a Non-billable call after the work order is invoiced — it is locked. Report it to the office instead.

Overview

1. RosterTick everyone who was on site today.
2. End timesConfirm or fix each forgotten clock-out.
3. Who gets billedUntick only the men the customer should not pay for; pick the reason.
4. SaveBOS completes the job and sums the billable hours.

Pre-Checks

  • The work is finished, or the job is being closed for the day with the supervisor's agreement.
  • Everyone on the crew has clocked out, or will be closed by the roster at sign-off.
  • You know whether this customer agreed a set price or budget.

Steps

1Open the sign-off formCrew lead
  • On the work order page scroll to the Sign-Off section and open the form for your crew type.
  • The form shows the work order, the roster field and the man-hours block.
2Fill the rosterCrew lead
  • Tick every person who worked on site today — including anyone borrowed from another crew.
  • On complex jobs (sprinkler, landscape) click Refresh man hours after changing the roster so the blocks below update.
3Confirm end timesCrew lead
  • Under Confirm end times for forgotten clock-outs, enter the time each listed person actually finished. The prefilled value is an estimate.
  • People on the roster with no clock entry get one created when you save, using the crew's times — tell the office if theirs was different.
4Read the target line (cleanups)Crew lead
  • Fall and spring cleanups show Target X hrs · Clocked so far Y hrs. It is information for the supervisor review — never a reason to change anyone's hours.
5Decide who gets billedCrew lead
  • In Who gets billed, every person starts as billed. Leave it that way unless the customer should not pay for that person's time.
  • For each person whose time is not charged: tick Non-billable and pick the reason — Over-staffed · Customer budget · Training · Rework · Other.
  • The hours stay on the job and on the teammate's record. Only the invoice leaves them out. Your name and the time are recorded with the call.
6Finish and saveCrew lead
  • Complete the rest of the form (trucks taken, notes, photos as your crew's form requires) and Save.
  • BOS sets the work order to Complete, closes the clock entries, and sums the billable hours into Total Time; non-billable hours go to Non-billable Time.

⚠ A reason is required

A Non-billable tick without a reason will not save. "Other" is for cases the list does not cover — not for skipping the question.

✕ Not a tool for hiding time

Supervisors review every non-billable hour by reason and by teammate on the Unbilled Hours report. A pattern of Non-billable ticks on ordinary days will be asked about.

Quality Checks

  • The work order shows status Complete.
  • The Hours section shows one card per person; each non-billable card carries a grey Non-billable badge with the reason and your name.
  • The Total line under the cards reads "X hrs non-billable" when any time was marked.
  • Nobody who worked today is missing from the Hours section.

Completion

  • Nothing else to do — the office sees the call on the work order and on the billing views.
  • If you made a mistake, tell the supervisor or office the same day. They can correct it while the job is Complete (OFF-ADM-NBL-001). Once invoiced it is locked.

Do / Don't

✕ Don't✓ Do
Sign off from the shop the next morning from memorySign off on site, the same day, while the crew is in front of you
Untick a man because the job ran longUntick a man only when the customer should not pay for him — and say why
Leave a budget job fully billed and let the office "sort it out"Mark the extra hours Non-billable · Customer budget so the invoice is right the first time
Tell the customer what they will be chargedLeave billing to the office; the sign-off is your record, not a quote
